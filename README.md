# MANIFEST — Contemporary Menswear Label

> Elevated, understated menswear originating in Kerala. Built from honest natural fibers, engineered with relaxed precision, and made to define your presence.

---

## Overview

**MANIFEST** is a contemporary menswear and retail destination in Kerala, India. Rejecting fast-fashion disposable trends, the brand emphasizes architectural silhouettes, heavyweight natural textiles, and personalized 1:1 human connection through physical counters and digital concierge lines.

## Key Website Features

* **Pure Typographic Identity**: Minimalist, monochromatic luxury aesthetic powered by geometric Montserrat typography.
* **Mobile-First App Experience**:
  * **App-Style Sticky Bottom Bar**: Quick one-thumb access to Home, Stores, Instagram, VIP Club, and an illuminated central Concierge button.
  * **Zero Floating Overlaps**: Replaced obstructive floating FABs with an ergonomic, safe-area-padded bottom navigation bar.
  * **Segmented Boutique Switcher**: iOS-style segmented toggle to switch between *All Boutiques*, *Mukkam Flagship*, and *Manjeri Outlet*.
  * **Horizontal Snap Touch Carousel**: Horizontal swipe rail for official Instagram channels with animated swipe guidance.
  * **Live Hours Status**: Live operational pulse indicators with direct one-tap WhatsApp store lines and Google Maps coordinates.
* **Accessibility & SEO**: Complete Schema.org JSON-LD Local Business & Organization structured data, XML sitemap (`sitemap.xml`), crawler directives (`robots.txt`), Open Graph & Twitter Cards, and geo-targeted indexing for Kerala search terms (*manifest fashion, manifest wedding, manifest manjeri, manifest mukkam, groom rentals, groom fits, groom custom, dress code*).

## Tech Stack

* **HTML5**: Semantic, accessible document structure.
* **CSS3**: Vanilla modern CSS with custom design tokens, safe area insets, backdrop blur filters, and fluid responsive typography (`clamp`).
* **JavaScript**: Modern ES6+ for reactive store filtering, scroll tracking, and navigation interactions without external libraries or bloat.

## Local Development

npx serve .
